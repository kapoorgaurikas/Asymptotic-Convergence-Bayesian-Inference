Interactive visualizations demonstrating the Law of Large Numbers (LLN), Central Limit Theorem (√n scaling), and Beta-Binomial Posterior Updating.

EXERCISE 01 SIMULATOR

Law of Large Numbers & Central Limit Theorem

Observe how the sample mean X̄n converges to μ, while rescaling by √n stabilizes variance at 1 and converges in distribution to N(0, 1).

LLN: Sample Mean Path (X̄n → μ)
CLT: Zn = √n(X̄n − μ)/σ →d N(0,1)
